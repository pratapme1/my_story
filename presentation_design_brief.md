# Presentation Project Summary and Design Brief

## Project Purpose

This project is an interactive React/Vite presentation built for a Dell SCA TPM case and scenario interview. The goal is to present Vishnu Pratap Kumar as a hands-on product and program leader who finds unclear enterprise problems, proves solutions with data and prototypes, and builds scalable systems across complex stakeholder environments.

The presentation is not only a resume walkthrough. It is designed to demonstrate a working style:

> Search. Prove. Build.

## Core Story

The story begins with enterprise complexity: 100K+ employees, thousands of software assets, fragmented ownership, slow request paths, and limited visibility into software usage and compliance. Vishnu identifies the hidden patterns, connects isolated workstreams, validates solutions with data and prototypes, and converts them into measurable enterprise outcomes.

The closing idea applies that same operating model to Dell SCA: instead of only describing a supplier-risk concept, the presentation includes an illustrative supplier risk dashboard that shows how structured signals can become analyst-ready actions.

## Main Message

Vishnu is strongest when the problem is messy, ownership is unclear, data is fragmented, and no complete playbook exists. He can move from ambiguity to evidence, from evidence to prototype, and from prototype to operational program.

## Visual Design Directions

### Cinematic Executive
- Dark background, gold accents, serif headlines, restrained motion.
- Best for a premium, high-stakes interview feel.
- Use large numbers, slow reveals, and minimal text.

### Enterprise Dashboard
- Clean grid, compact cards, risk tables, status chips, gauges, and action queues.
- Best for showing SCA relevance and operational thinking.
- Use neutral backgrounds with red, amber, green, and blue signal colors.

### Data Investigation
- Terminal-like code boxes, SQL/search logs, data bars, timelines, and evidence cards.
- Best for showing the "Search" and "Prove" parts of the story.
- Make the viewer feel the presenter discovered the truth through data.

### Program Transformation
- Flow diagrams showing three separate workstreams converging into one program.
- Best for explaining leadership, program thinking, and business impact.
- Use connected lines, workstream cards, and a strong final outcome metric.

### Case-Interview Ready
- Simple slides with one question, one framework, and one recommendation.
- Best for speaking clearly in the 15-minute presentation window.
- Favor plain language over decorative visuals.

## Suggested Design Prompts

### Prompt 1: Cinematic Opening
Create a dark cinematic executive presentation opening for a Dell SCA TPM interview. Use the words "Search. Prove. Build." as the central identity. Style should feel premium, minimal, and confident, with serif typography, subtle gold accents, and restrained motion.

### Prompt 2: Data Discovery Slide
Design a slide showing an enterprise product manager discovering hidden software platform problems through data. Include evidence cards for 14.3s search latency reduced to 3.1s, 365K software assets, and fragmented ownership. Use a dark technical aesthetic with terminal-style details.

### Prompt 3: Program Impact Slide
Design a slide where three workstreams converge into one CIO program: Software Catalog, Compliance Agent, and Deployment Engine. Show the outcome as a projected $400M initiative with supporting metrics: 100K+ users, 98K endpoints, and 70% cycle-time reduction.

### Prompt 4: Supplier Risk Dashboard
Create an enterprise supplier risk dashboard for Dell SCA. Show suppliers, tiers, countries, risk scores, structured risk signals, recommended actions, and high/medium/low statuses. The design should be practical, analyst-focused, and action-oriented.

### Prompt 5: Closing Vision
Design a closing slide that connects Vishnu's working method to Dell SCA: "Search. Prove. Build. Applied to your data and your analysts." Make it feel calm, credible, and executive-ready.
